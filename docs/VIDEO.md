Working title: **A Gesture Across Eight Centuries**. Target read: approximately 55 seconds, including short pauses. Present-tense project language below expresses intent; no successful robot pour is asserted. Record the footage first and keep the outcome caption factual.

## Voiceover (121 words)

> More than eight centuries ago, Al-Jazari described machines that poured and served drinks. One surviving manuscript shows a figure pouring wine from a bottle into a cup.
>
> I want to revisit that gesture with a ROBOTIS AIWorker: one bottle, one cup, and a pour we can measure.
>
> The connection is a question about making. Water flows through a mechanism. Electric current powers a motor. A learned policy can turn observations into actions. These are different systems, brought together here as an analogy.
>
> We begin with a dry rehearsal, then measure what reaches the cup and what spills. Every clip will say whether the motion is teleoperated, scripted, or learned.
>
> Across the centuries, people keep giving ideas a body—and seeing what happens.

Historical lines: [David Collection, inventory 20/1988](https://catalog.davidmus.dk/en/item/1542/20-1988.pdf). The learned-policy line describes a possible control method, not this project's current achievement. The current branch is simulation recovery.
