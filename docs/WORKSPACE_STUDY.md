## Fit and evaluation design

The candidate retained the previous neural encoders and feedback controller. Training fit the expanded bottle/cup templates, the settled-height ridge head and the contextual pouring movement primitive. Search bounds extended to every retained template family. New images and joint targets came from reference demonstrations, with the completed C30 batch available as consumed development evidence. No W30 development or reserved test trajectories entered the height or movement fit.

The first fit was stopped before checkpoint creation because the old median-template matching missed or mislocalized the wider bottle views. Its 52 calibration windows and 18 rejected consensus candidates are retained in `fit-attempt0-localization-candidates.json`; no reserved data were inspected. A CPU ablation compared signed/unsigned gradients and median/maximum matching. The selected unsigned-gradient maximum, restricted to 28 pixels from each stored training-view anchor during initialization, localized all 52 first images within 0.615 mm. Positive scores were at least 0.7872 versus a maximum synthetic-occlusion score of 0.6352; the source confidence anchor increases to 0.70. This is calibration evidence, not independent task accuracy. Forty source templates use 24 consumed C30 training views and 16 corner reference views; cup matching adds 16 maximum-matched templates to its retained family. Validation images were excluded from the templates. The first full-window fit still failed: one retained C30 view repeatedly selected a reflection 10.48 mm from the rim. That fit is archived separately. A calibrated ensemble combines the retained median matcher with the new outline matcher: retained score becomes `0.70 + 0.75 × (score - 0.40)`, while outline scores stay unchanged; choose the larger score. These are comparison scores, not probabilities. A replay of all 52 windows meets the 2 mm requirement (maximum 0.583 mm) and rejects all 52 synthetic occlusions (maximum score 0.6374). Twenty-five maps and all rejected spatial-penalty variants are retained. Only initialization uses score calibration; lifted-bottle verification keeps raw scores. Thirty native CPU checks pass. The combined fit completed and passed native full-window calibration with the same 0.583 mm maximum error. Checkpoint `ea5bf6177c02ab03987ce2fa4bb01d88327541c2ea9a4ec1fc7976f871913e64` and all 29 sources are archived under `candidates/v18-r0`. Calibration alone did not count as an autonomous success.

The settled-height fit uses 98 training / 28 validation episodes, 3,412 / 960 frames, 16 coefficients and ridge lambda .001. Near-full validation MAE is 0.951 percentage points, with 95th-percentile overestimate 1.643 points; the feedback target is 92.143% predicted settled rim height. Motion imitation uses 35 training / 8 validation demonstrations, with per-joint validation RMSE 0.058–0.081 degrees. The frozen empty detector passes its frame-level empty/presence threshold on 21/28 labeled-empty validation frames and 1/86 nonempty frames; sparse offline replay does not establish closed-loop empty-stop reliability. The subsequent development rounds tested this in simulation.

Method: supervised regression / behavioral cloning plus visual servo and feedback control. Settled height minimizes episode-weighted squared prediction error plus ridge regularization. The motion model minimizes joint-target imitation error conditioned on measured lift joints and localized cup position. Simulator poses/particles supply offline labels and evaluation scores; runtime control receives RGB, joints, own history and fixed calibration.

Candidate selection uses development evidence. Only afterward are the checkpoint, source hashes and plan frozen and the thirty reserved cases run once, with fifteen full and fifteen empty targets. All failures remain in the denominator. No tuning, retries, altered thresholds or teacher fallback are permitted during that batch.

For visual review, inspect task and wrist frames at the stop and final release for every reserved case. The two example videos use the first planned full target (`W30_A_test_00`) and first planned empty target (`W30_A_test_01`), irrespective of their scores. `tools/plot_workspace_outcomes.py` plots the actual whole-task outcome at each specified start pose, final cup height and bottle remainder. `tools/watch_workspace_trials.py --part test --record-test-snapshots` records the fixed model, sources, container limits and GPU process inventory after 5, 15, 25 and 30 closed tests.

The unchanged strict goal requires verified sustained grasp, liquid transfer, correct stop reason, full upright return/release, at least 90% final rim height or an empty source, zero final outside particles and zero sampled near-table loss. Transient outside readings are reported separately. The 20 Hz loss observations do not exclude all splashes or between-sample excursions. Nominal mL remains an uncalibrated particle-count quantity; the legacy nominal-volume score stays separate.
